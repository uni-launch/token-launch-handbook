# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a documentation repository about launching tokens, not a
place to argue, promote or recruit.

## Expected

- Straightforward, respectful language, including in disagreement.
- Corrections aimed at the text, not at the person who wrote it.
- Patience with beginners. Everyone here was one recently.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Promotion of tokens, projects, presales or "opportunities".
- Asking anyone for a seed phrase, private key or wallet access, in any
  context, for any stated reason. This results in an immediate and permanent
  ban, and the content is reported.
- Impersonating the maintainers or support.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaches this through
[support](https://unilaunch.online/open-ticket) or by opening an issue if it
can be discussed publicly. Reports are handled privately where they need to be.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that breach this document,
and may block accounts that repeat the behaviour.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
