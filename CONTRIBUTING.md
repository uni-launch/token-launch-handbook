# Contributing

This handbook is maintained by the team behind
[Unilaunch](https://unilaunch.online), and corrections from outside are the
most valuable thing it receives. A wrong fee, an outdated chain list or a dead
link makes the whole thing less trustworthy.

## What is welcome

- **Corrections.** Anything factually wrong or out of date. Please link a
  source where one exists.
- **Clarity fixes.** A sentence that confused you probably confuses others.
- **New guides**, if they answer a question a first-time launcher actually
  asks and do not simply restate an existing page.
- **Translations.** Open an issue first so two people do not translate the same
  page twice.

## What is out of scope

- Promotion of tokens, projects or competing tools.
- Price predictions, trading advice, or anything that reads as financial
  advice.
- Copies of content from the main site — the guides here are written to stand
  on their own and to link to the site, not to duplicate it.

## How

1. Fork, branch, edit the Markdown.
2. Keep the house style: plain sentences, no marketing language, tables for
   anything comparative, and a link the first time a concept appears.
3. Open a pull request describing what changed and why. The link checker runs
   automatically.

## Questions about your own launch

Those belong in [support](https://unilaunch.online/open-ticket), not in issues
here — support can see your wallet and your transaction, and this repository
cannot.
