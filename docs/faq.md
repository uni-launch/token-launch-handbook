# Short FAQ

Longer answers, kept current, live at
[unilaunch.online/faq](https://unilaunch.online/faq?utm_source=github&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=handbook).

**Do I need to know how to code to launch a token?**
No. Connect a wallet, fill in the details, confirm the transaction from your
own wallet. That is the whole flow.

**Which chains can I launch on today?**
Solana, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Blast and
Robinhood Chain. Monad and Tempo are being integrated. See
[Choosing a chain](choose-a-chain.md).

**Which wallets work?**
Phantom and Solflare on Solana; MetaMask and Trust Wallet on the EVM chains.

**Is Unilaunch a custodian?**
No. It is software: it builds transactions, your wallet signs them, and the
chain executes them. It never holds keys, tokens or liquidity, and never asks
for a seed phrase.

**What does it cost?**
A tool fee in the chain's native coin, plus network gas, plus whatever
liquidity you choose to deposit. Numbers in [Fees and costs](fees-and-costs.md).

**Can I change the token after launch?**
Mostly no. Name, symbol and decimals are permanent unless you deliberately kept
the update authority on Solana; supply is fixed once the mint authority is
revoked. See [Token authorities](token-authorities.md).

**Can I add liquidity later?**
Yes. Positions live in
[My Portfolio](https://unilaunch.online/liquidity-pool?utm_source=github&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=handbook), where liquidity can be
added or removed — including for a token launched without a pool.

**What if I send a transaction with the wrong details?**
Nothing can be done: confirmed transactions are irreversible by anyone. This is
why the [checklist](launch-checklist.md) exists.

**Where do I get help?**
Open a ticket at
[unilaunch.online/open-ticket](https://unilaunch.online/open-ticket?utm_source=github&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=handbook). Support
never initiates contact by DM.
